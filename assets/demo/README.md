# Demo album covers

Original artwork for LavaTUI's invented `--demo` songs, one sleeve per
album. Generated with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst through Venice
(`gpt-image-2-5-sunburst`), then each reduced to a 400 × 400 JPEG. The
exact prompts and generation settings are in [prompts.json](prompts.json).
No existing artwork was supplied.

| File | Used for (album) | Printed artist / album | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `suspended-melt.jpg` | Heat Rises (The Paraffins), and the made-up library's other albums | Wax and Wane / Suspended Melt | Sage and coral wax blobs inside a tapered lamp outline, with risograph grain |
| `waxwood-hymns.jpg` | Lamplight (Wax and Wane) | Wax and Wane / Waxwood Hymns | A dark linocut lava-lamp silhouette with ochre wax rising inside, on cream paper |

`suspended-melt.jpg` was cover 09 in the user's candidate selection and
`waxwood-hymns.jpg` cover 06. The songs' existing titles, artists, albums
and lyrics stay unchanged, so a sleeve's printed album name differs from
the playing track's metadata. The images were resized with no colour
adjustment. The art is embedded in the binary; demo playback does not call
Venice or download it.
